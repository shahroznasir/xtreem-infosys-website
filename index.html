Client FAQ

Frequently Answered Questions

Essential details regarding SLA response guarantees, resident staffing deployment, OEM platform coverage, and spare part logistics.

How fast is your technical emergency dispatch across Delhi NCR?

Under our Comprehensive Enterprise SLA, critical server and core network infrastructure emergencies receive on-premise dispatch within 2 to 4 hours anywhere across South Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, and Greater NCR. Standby replacement loaner hardware is deployed simultaneously to ensure business continuity.

Can Xtreem Infosys deploy full-time resident engineers (FMS) at our premises?

Yes. We provide certified, background-verified L1, L2, and L3 Facility Management (FMS) engineers dedicated exclusively to your offices. They manage daily user helpdesk requests, hardware asset inventory barcodes, boardroom AV setups, and executive VIP support during your standard operating shifts.

What OEM hardware brands and platforms are supported under your contracts?

We deliver multi-vendor hardware engineering across Dell PowerEdge & Latitude, HP Enterprise & ProLiant, Lenovo ThinkPad & ThinkSystem, Cisco routing/switching, Apple MacBook & iMac fleets, Fortinet firewalls, and Epson/BenQ high-lumen boardroom projectors.

Are replacement components 100% genuine, and do you supply on-premise loaner spares?

Every replaced component—from power supply units (PSUs) and SAS RAID controllers to laptop displays and motherboard ICs—is a 100% genuine certified part. In comprehensive plans, essential standby spare units are staged directly on your premises so zero downtime occurs while repairs are completed.
